# Risk register (prototype)

**Status: started 3 Oct 2026, updated with every slice (last: V3, 4 Oct 2026).** An engineering working record in the spirit of hazard analysis (ISO 14971 practice); **not** a completed risk-management file, and not evidence of compliance. Severity and likelihood are my qualitative judgement, to be reviewed by a clinical safety owner. Residual risk is what remains after the controls *that exist and are tested today*; "planned" controls are not counted.

Severity: **H** harm to a patient is plausible; **M** wrong or missing information that a clinician would normally catch; **L** inconvenience or privacy exposure of synthetic data only. Controls marked ✔ are implemented and tested; ◻ are planned (slice in brackets).

| ID | Hazard | Cause | Severity | Controls | Residual |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| R1 | AI text recorded as the clinician's own decision | AI output inserted or signed without review | H | ✔ drafts only; accepted text enters the note only by a recorded insertion with provenance; ✔ nothing is auto-signed (tested); ✔ AI contractually cannot draft diagnoses or prescriptions; ✔ **the V3 consultation draft keeps the same rule**: Accept, Edit and Reject record a decision and change nothing else, "Add to note" is a separate click that appends the reviewed text to a note section and never replaces what the doctor wrote, there is no route from a draft to the diagnoses, the prescription or an order, and a flagged statement needs an explicit tick enforced by the server (tested) | Low if the clinician reads what they accept; automation bias remains (R25) |
| R2 | Wrong or invented clinical statement from the model | Model error, small local models, transcription errors | H | ✔ statements cite sources and uncited ones are flagged; ✔ flagged facts need explicit confirmation (server-enforced); ✔ deterministic allergy/record-conflict flags; ✔ synthetic gold set, harness and model card ([AI_MODELS.md](AI_MODELS.md)) — **held-out recall of the important-point rules is 53 % (precision 75 %): it misses things**; ✔ every transcript line carries the model score and "Low model score"; ✔ **V3 consultation draft: every statement about the record must cite items that exist (the rest are removed); a figure, dose, medicine or wording that is in no cited item is removed or marked; the support state (supported / inferred / not found / conflicting / outdated) is decided by code, not by the model; "insufficient information" is a valid answer; what the checks removed is counted on the draft and shown** (tested with answers that invent; [AI_MODELS.md](AI_MODELS.md) counts what a real local model did on 12 synthetic cases, with the checks' effect; **no accuracy is claimed**) | Real. Measured only on synthetic scripts, synthetic speech and 12 synthetic cases; **nothing is measured on real consultations**, and a wrong statement that is worded plausibly and cites a real item cannot be recognised by code (R25) |
| R3 | Doctor misses an allergy | Allergy not shown or not prominent in the consultation | H | ✔ allergies with **severity** in the consultation banner (red for severe), always shown in the queue and the dashboard rows, repeated in the past-history panel and the summary; the banner falls back to the consultation's own allergy names while the record loads or fails, **and says "Allergy list may be out of date" when a refresh fails**; ✔ the list is re-read every minute and on window focus; ✔ "severity not recorded" is amber, not neutral; ✔ the allergies are repeated where medicines are chosen and in the review-and-sign step, and (name, MRN, allergies) stay with the consultation bar once the banner has scrolled away; ✔ allergy rules in recommendations | Low-medium: depends on allergies having been recorded (no feed); "allergies not recorded" is shown as a warning, not as none |
| R4 | Doctor decides without relevant history | Conditions, medicines, results not recorded or not shown | H | ✔ longitudinal history model (conditions, medicines, results, earlier signed consultations) read in one place and shown in the consultation ([ADR 0013](../adr/0013-patient-history-typed-by-staff-never-deleted.md)); ✔ entries typed by staff, **each shown with who and when**; ✔ the newest result of every test is always listed, however many results one test has; ✔ a result's flag is computed by the server and a number that disagrees with the result as it reads is refused; ◻ no laboratory or pharmacy feed, no reconciliation step | Medium: the history is only as complete as people make it, and the screen says it is typed in |
| R5 | Wrong patient | Similar names, wrong record opened | H | ✔ name, age, sex, MRN and blood group in the consultation banner with condition and allergy chips and a *Synthetic* tag from the data; ✔ facility scope; ✔ a page is rebuilt when a different person signs in; ◻ photo | Low-medium |
| R6 | Stale or unverified device value taken as current | No time or source shown; no live/stale state | M-H | ✔ readings stored as *device unverified*; ✔ every vital sign tile shows its time and whether it was typed by staff or came from a device (unverified); ◻ live / stale / disconnected / simulated states [V5] | Medium: no live state exists, and no device is integrated |
| R7 | Blood pressure shown from an unvalidated pipeline | Device gateway accepts BP kinds | M-H | ✔ BP is manual entry only in the product today; ◻ allow-list of device kinds, no BP without a validated pipeline [V5] | Low-medium: the gateway gap is untested |
| R8 | Simulated data mistaken for real | Simulator or synthetic data unlabelled | H | ✔ global "synthetic data only" banner; ✔ synthetic names; ✔ `data_origin` on patients and every history entry, shown as a tag; the hospital seed refuses to run in production, outside a synthetic deployment, outside a facility named "(synthetic)" or beside any patient marked clinical; ✔ an entry takes its origin from its patient, and a production deployment must state `DATA_ORIGIN` (the server will not start otherwise); ◻ labelled simulators, never presented as a device [V5] | Low-medium: older patients are `unspecified` |
| R9 | Thermal output read as a diagnosis | Flags look like findings | M-H | ✔ "not a diagnosis / not validated" banner and badges; ✔ rules only, no model claims; ◻ measured / model / clinician separation, quality and uncertainty [V6] | Low-medium |
| R10 | Patient data sent to a third-party AI service | A hosted key set; audio cannot be pseudonymised | M (synthetic data today); H (real data) | ✔ **speech-to-text is local only**: no hosted adapter in the code, default provider `none`, loopback enforced, no fallback ([ADR 0014](../adr/0014-local-speech-service-and-capture-pipeline.md)), so **consultation audio never leaves this computer**; ✔ important points are local rules; ✔ **hosted AI egress is a server policy that is OFF by default and a provider key alone enables nothing** (`ai.egress.hostedProviders`, `AI_EGRESS_HOSTED`; no hosted client is even constructed with it off; the browser can only read the state; request-counting tests prove zero calls with a key present) ([ADR 0015](../adr/0015-hosted-ai-egress-policy-and-speech-service-hardening.md)); ✔ when allowed, the screen says where text goes and that audio never does; ✔ every run records local or external; ✔ per-feature consent gates and the doctor's click; ✔ best-effort text scrubbing; ✔ test config carries no keys and no test calls a real provider; ✔ **V3: consultation drafts go only to the language model on this computer** (loopback, a per-install secret, no network of its own); a down local model gives "Local AI unavailable." and is **never** replaced by a hosted one; a hosted reasoning mode exists only if the server is set to it, with egress on and a key, and is `none`-able; request-counting tests prove zero hosted requests with a key present and egress off or on and the local model down ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-local-ai-consultation-intelligence.md)) | **Low while egress stays off (the default).** If it is turned on: Medium (synthetic data) / High (real data): pseudonymisation is best effort; **whether egress may ever be turned on for real data is a governance decision**
| R11 | Unauthorised access to records | Role or facility bypass; stolen session | H | ✔ default-deny routes, facility scope, consent enforcement, denial audit; ✔ lockout, rate limits, server sessions; ✔ per-door application check; ✘ no MFA, no TLS (local single machine); ✘ no care-team scoping inside a facility | Medium for a real deployment; out of scope for the prototype |
| R12 | Record altered after signing | Direct database change | H | ✔ immutability triggers; ✔ hash-linked amendments; ✔ verification; ✔ audit hash chain, append-only privileges; unkeyed fingerprint noted as a limit | Low-medium |
| R13 | Lost or unreviewed edits | Autosave failure, concurrent edit, session change mid-edit | M | ✔ serial autosave with version check, conflict stops editing; ✔ a pending save is never sent as a different person | Low |
| R14 | Doctor cannot tell what is draft, confirmed or measured | Mixed presentation | M-H | ✔ draft badges and review states; ✔ the consultation says **"Draft · in progress"**; ✔ the patient summary says it is composed from the record, not AI; ✔ "prescribed earlier" is separate from the medicine list and labelled; ✔ **consultation text is visibly raw transcript → extracted draft → doctor confirmed (or rejected)** in three different treatments, and confirming never writes to the note (V2, [DESIGN.md](../DESIGN.md) §4c); ✔ **the V3 draft is labelled AI DRAFT / AI-GENERATED / REQUIRES DOCTOR REVIEW, every statement shows its support state and the record items it rests on, and a draft is marked STALE when the record moved**; ◻ measured / model / clinician labelling for devices and images [V5, V6] | Low-medium for consultation text and drafts; Medium for devices and images |
| R15 | Transcription in the wrong language or poor quality is trusted | No language identification, no confidence | M | ✔ language (with how it was decided), model score, times and the model that heard each line; ✔ "Low model score" below 0.5; ✔ original text always kept, never translated; ✔ the doctor assigns speakers; ✔ lines in another language are skipped by the extractor and counted on screen; ✔ English only is supported | Medium: measured only on clean synthetic English speech; Hindi, Tamil and code-mixed speech were **not** evaluated |
| R16 | Usability error under time pressure | Dense screen, small targets, alarm fatigue | M | ✔ 44 px targets, axe checks, keyboard use, no fake controls; ◻ usability scenarios and formative checks [V3] | Not assessed |
| R17 | Audit trail incomplete for a consultation | Draft saves, prints, reads not visible to the doctor | M | ✔ admin audit log and chain verification; ◻ per-consultation trail, print audit event [V7, V8] | Medium |
| R18 | A mis-heard word becomes a "fact" (a drug name, a number, a dropped "no") | Speech model error; sentences cut at the 20-second part boundaries | H | ✔ the raw transcript sits beside the points, each point names its source lines with their times; ✔ points are drafts until the doctor confirms; ✔ a sentence cut where a part ended is read whole (lines are stitched) or flagged "cut where a recording part ended" and then needs the explicit tick; ✔ negation and hedging kept; ✘ no check of drug names against a formulary | Medium-high on real audio (not measured; synthetic speech scored 1.8 % word error) |
| R19 | A statement attributed to the wrong speaker | No model identifies speakers; the rules guess from the wording | M-H | ✔ no automated diarisation is claimed; the doctor assigns Doctor / Patient / … line by line (append-only review); ✔ a point whose side was guessed says so ("Speaker not assigned: guessed patient") and needs an explicit tick, enforced by the server; ✔ a point whose source line is later assigned to the other side is stale and needs the tick again (server and screen); ✔ points from a relative or attendant are flagged | Low-medium while the doctor reads the lines; Medium if they accept without reading |
| R20 | Audio or text captured without, or after withdrawal of, consent | Consent changes during a recording; races | H | ✔ consent checked at start, on every part (before the body is read and again before it is stored), on upload, on retry, on extraction and again after the speech model returns, so nothing is stored for a part transcribed after withdrawal; ✔ the browser polls and stops the microphone and discards the part in progress; ✔ the end reason is derived from the consent, not the browser's say-so; ✔ the visit state is checked too (a paused visit refuses parts); ✔ **a recording in progress is ended in the withdrawal itself** (audited), not only by the browser; ✔ the screens state that **nothing already captured is deleted or hidden** ([CAPTURE_DATA.md](CAPTURE_DATA.md)); ◻ audio and text recorded **before** withdrawal are kept (no purge: a governance decision); the consent wording changed in V2 and **needs a legal and clinical-governance review** | Low-medium; owner decision on what withdrawal should delete |
| R21 | Consultation audio and transcripts kept too long, or read by the wrong role | No retention schedule for discarded consultations; evidence tables copy text | M | ✔ playback of consultation audio is doctor-only (nurses get "not found", audited); ✔ facility scope; ✔ retention for signed consultations; ✘ a discarded consultation keeps its audio; ✘ transcript text is copied into append-only evidence tables and the facility-wide review counts; ✘ audio is not encrypted at rest (local disk); ✘ nothing is purged on withdrawal | Medium; **retention, erasure, encryption and who may read captures are governance decisions that are not made** ([CAPTURE_DATA.md](CAPTURE_DATA.md) lists exactly what is stored and where) |
| R22 | The local speech service is abused | Any local process can call it; hostile audio; DNS rebinding; resource exhaustion | L-M | ✔ loopback only (the launcher refuses another address); ✔ requests to a non-loopback host name refused; ✔ a client header a browser cannot send cross-origin without a pre-flight; ✔ uploads kept in memory, logs withhold exception text; ✔ allow-listed environment; ✔ **content decides**: only WebM, Ogg, WAV, MP3 or MP4 by their first bytes and matching the declared type, refused (415) by the API before storing and again by the service; ✔ **the demuxer is chosen by us with an in-memory-only protocol whitelist** (an upload cannot make the service open a file or an address); ✔ **a duration bound before decoding** (declared length, or a stream that stops at the bound; default 3,600 s); ✔ size 64 MB, enforced **before the body is parsed** (a declared length over the limit is refused unread; a body that declares none is cut off at the limit), and at most 4 requests pending, **counted before the body is read** (429 + Retry-After); ✔ per-user API rate limits on audio and costly steps (429, audited); ✘ **no sign-in, by design** (localhost sidecar: anything on the same computer can use it; a remote deployment would need a network boundary and real authentication that do not exist); ✘ unsandboxed FFmpeg under the user's account | Low-medium |
| R23 | A tampered or wrong model or package is loaded | Download and dependency supply chain | M | ✔ pinned model revision, file sizes and SHA-256 checked at download **and the weights checked against the pinned hash at every load**; ✔ the weights hash is stored on every run; ✔ the download is explicit and asks first; ✔ pinned package versions; ✘ package hashes are not pinned | Low-medium |
| R24 | Part of a consultation is silently missing | A part fails to transcribe, upload or is lost; the system stops mid-job | M | ✔ a part that failed shows "could not be transcribed" with Retry and the audio kept; ✔ steps left running by a stop are failed (retryable) at the next start; ✔ a recording ended elsewhere is reported; ✘ a part lost after the browser's four retries is a transient warning, and after a reload nothing in the transcript marks the gap | Medium |

| R25 | A plausible, wrong AI statement is accepted (automation bias, anchoring on the AI's considerations) | Fluent text; a doctor under time pressure; a statement that cites a real record item for something the item does not say | H | ✔ every statement shows the items it rests on, open to the doctor beside it; ✔ "AI DRAFT / REQUIRES DOCTOR REVIEW"; ✔ support states and marks (inferred, not found, conflicting, outdated); ✔ considerations are phrased "Consider…", carry no dose and no medicine that is not in the record, and are never orders; ✔ nothing reaches the note, the diagnoses or the prescription without the separate "Add to note"; ✔ accepting, editing and rejecting are recorded per statement (who, when, what); ◻ no usability test, no measure of how often a wrong statement is accepted | **Medium-high.** Code cannot recognise a wrong statement that cites a real item and reads well. **Not measured with any clinician** |
| R26 | A missing item is read as "nothing there" (false reassurance) | The model leaves out an allergy, condition or medicine that the record holds, or lists as missing what the record has; a draft with few marks looks complete | M-H | ✔ the fixed record check (conflicts, old items, what is not recorded) is shown with every draft and **without** a model, labelled "fixed rules, not AI"; ✔ allergies stay in the consultation banner whatever the draft says; ✔ an absence the record contradicts is marked and the item is shown; ✔ the draft says how many older items did not fit and were not shown; ✔ coverage of what the record plainly says is counted in the evaluation (no accuracy claimed); ✘ nothing detects an *omission* in the draft beyond that | Medium |
| R27 | An instruction hidden in record text steers the model | A patient-written note, a pasted letter or an earlier note contains "ignore your instructions…" | M-H | ✔ the model has **no tools, no network and cannot act**: its answer is only text for the doctor; ✔ sources are one line each with brackets neutralised, the task comes after the sources, the prompt says to ignore instructions in sources; ✔ the answer is shape-checked, link-stripped and checked for figures, doses, medicines and citations, so an obeyed "recommend amoxicillin 500 mg" or "no allergies" is removed; ✔ tested with answers that obey it and with a real model on a case that contains one (the model did not obey; see [AI_MODELS.md](AI_MODELS.md)) | Low-medium: a source can still make the model write a *misleading but cited* sentence, which the doctor sees beside its source |
| R28 | A draft made from an out-of-date record is used | The doctor edits the note, confirms more points or the record changes after the draft was made | M | ✔ the manifest of what the draft was made from is compared with the record on every read: **STALE** with one sentence saying what changed, and "changed since" on each affected statement; ✔ nothing regenerates by itself; what the doctor adds to the note from the draft is not counted as a change; ✔ drafts are versioned and kept | Low-medium |
| R29 | Patient context reaches the model that the doctor may not see | A wrong patient or facility, an unreviewed or rejected point, the raw transcript, another doctor's draft | H | ✔ authorisation and consent are checked **before** anything is read for the model; ✔ one function builds the input, from the facility-scoped record loader (404 for another facility), the doctor's own draft and **only confirmed** points; ✔ the transcript and audio are never in it; ✔ name, MRN and phone are replaced; ✔ tested: nurse, another doctor, another facility, signed-out, signed or discarded consultation, no consent, withdrawn consent, rejected and unreviewed points | Low |
| R30 | The model or its server is replaced, tampered with or used by something else | Download supply chain; another process on the computer | M | ✔ model and runtime pinned by size and SHA-256, downloaded only by an explicit command that asks first and deletes a mismatch; ✔ the weights hash is recorded on every draft and the model's name on the screen; ✔ the server is offline, loopback, secret-protected, without web page or slot endpoints, with an allow-listed environment; ✔ the client refuses redirects and non-loopback addresses; ✘ **any process of the same user can read the secret file and call the server**; ✘ package-level hashes inside the runtime archive are not independently verified | Low-medium |
| R31 | A slow, stuck or failed draft is misread or leads to repeated requests | Minutes per draft on a CPU; a stopped model; a closed laptop | L-M | ✔ visible states (NOT RUN, GENERATING with elapsed time, READY, PARTIALLY AVAILABLE, FAILED, UNAVAILABLE, STALE) and a Cancel that stops the model call; ✔ one draft per consultation at a time and an idempotency key (no duplicate work); ✔ a timeout and a plain reason for each failure, with a retry; ✔ drafts left running by a stop are failed at the next start | Low |
| R32 | The "I have checked this" tick becomes a reflex | Many marked statements; a doctor in a hurry | M | ✔ a tick is asked only for specific reasons (a flag the checks raised, or a suggestion that cites nothing), and the reasons are shown; ✔ the sources are open beside the statement; ✘ the tick's effect on behaviour is **not measured** | Medium |
| R33 | Draft text is kept longer than it should be, or read by the wrong role | Drafts keep the excerpts of the record items they cite, in append-only evidence rows | M | ✔ the consultation's own doctor only (a nurse or another doctor of the facility gets 403, someone outside the facility 404, once the consultation is signed or discarded 409; **the review of a draft's statements is listed only for that doctor** in the AI review page and the administrators' counts carry no text); ✔ facility scope; ✔ identifiers replaced before the text is built; ✘ **no retention schedule, no erasure, nothing purged on withdrawal, no encryption at rest** (the draft is part of the audit record); whether a local model needs its own consent and wording is **not decided** | Medium; **governance decisions that are not made** ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-local-ai-consultation-intelligence.md) §3) |
| R34 | Earlier patients' records stay in the model server's memory, and an answer depends on what the server was asked before | The runtime's default prompt cache keeps the state of every earlier prompt for reuse; an operator starts the server by hand with the defaults | M | ✔ the launcher switches prompt caching off (`--no-cache-prompt --cache-ram 0 --no-cache-idle-slots --ctx-checkpoints 0`), checked by running it with `--dry-run`; ✔ **measured**: with the defaults the server's committed memory grew by about 160 MB per distinct prompt (2.2 GB to 6.0 GB over 24 prompts; 9.7 GB on the long-running development server) and none of the 12 answers of the first evaluation recording equals the second's; with the settings above memory stayed at 2.2 GB and two server instances with different histories gave identical answers for two cases; ✔ nothing is written to disk (no slot-save path), and the server's console output holds no prompt or answer text (checked with sentinel strings); ✘ the state of the *most recent* request stays in memory until the next one replaces it; ✘ **nothing stops an operator from starting the server by hand with the defaults**, or another process of the same user from reading its memory ([ADR 0016](../adr/0016-local-ai-consultation-intelligence.md) §2.1) | Low-medium |

**Review rule:** each slice adds or updates the rows it touches, and its STATUS entry says which controls were verified by which test.
